The four modules

Each module has one idea, one failure to avoid, and one discipline that fixes it. You work on a real presentation of your own throughout. Open a module to see what it covers.

1

Fit

Start with the audience's wants and needs, not the slides

 ▼
You leave with
An understanding of FIT, FOCUS & FLAIR, with the focus on FIT.
The failure
You know your subject so well you forget what it is like not to, and that can make it hard to relate it to an audience who cares about what it does for them.
The discipline
Find out about your audience before you write anything else.
2

Focus

One message, a three-act story, and a clear call to action

 ▼
You leave with
A plan to help you prepare for your presentation: what do you want them to Know, Understand, Do? Your core message outlined in a single sentence. A draft story structure: three acts with headlines for each.
The failure
Hiding the message in the detail. The point arrives on slide thirty-six, long after the room has drifted away.
The discipline
Be really focused on what your message means.
3

Flair · the message

Make it easy to remember

 ▼
You leave with
A 30-second prologue, a headline, a soundbite and an image for each of your three acts.
The failure
Writing for a reader instead of writing to be said out loud. Prose that reads well on the page is often hard to say and harder to hear.
The discipline
Say the words onto the page. Use active, direct words and language.
4

Flair · the slides

Create visuals to add impact, emphasis and illustration to your words

 ▼
You leave with
A slide plan and a bullet-point script. This is the draft you bring to Barcelona.
The failure
The slide as teleprompter. You read it, so the audience stops listening and reads it too.
The discipline
Build the script first, then decide what visuals you need.
The slide check
Three seconds to understand it. One idea per slide. One object at a time. The headline carries the meaning. Animation for emphasis.

Fit, Focus and Flair

Three pillars of a great presentation. Get all three right, and the message lands quickly and sticks afterwards. Miss one and the other two work harder for less.

Fit

The right message for this audience, in this room, on this day.

Focus

One big idea, carried by a three-act story, ending in a clear call to action.

Flair

The words, the images and the delivery that make people remember it tomorrow.

If you have not yet completed the Fit, Focus & Flair assessment, do it before the next module. It takes about ten minutes and gives you a personal profile across the three pillars. Open the assessment

Before you present, anything, ever

Six questions. If you cannot answer all six, the presentation is not ready.

  • Does the audience understand why this matters to them?
  • Can your argument be summarised in one sentence?
  • Do you have a simple three-act story?
  • Do you have a strong start and a clear call to action at the end?
  • Have you removed all the visual and verbal clutter?
  • Do your slides pass the 3-second test?
